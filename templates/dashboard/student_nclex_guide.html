What is NCLEX?

The NCLEX is the official licensing exam for nurses who want to practice in countries such as the US and Canada.

Exam Format
  • Computer Adaptive Testing (CAT)
  • Up to 5 hours
  • 75-145 questions
Question Types
  • Multiple Choice (MCQ)
  • Select All That Apply (SATA)
  • Fill in the Blank
  • Ordered Response / Drag and Drop
Pass Strategy
  1. Practice daily
  2. Prioritize ABC and safety
  3. Focus on rationales
  4. Take timed tests regularly
